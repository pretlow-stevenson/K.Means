About

AI systems built where simple answers break.

K Means AI is a high-velocity AI research and development lab engineering bespoke reasoning systems for complex, failure-prone work. We operate as a lean collective of engineers and researchers who favor architectural elegance over bloat, pushing frontier models to their limits to discover where stronger systems begin.

Every line of code is treated as load-bearing: precise, intentional, and shaped by a minimalist’s obsession with efficiency.

How We Work

Small teams, sharp systems, measurable behavior.

We prototype quickly, then harden around observed failure modes. Every system is shaped by traces: where models disagreed, which assumptions changed the outcome, and what evidence supported the final result.

The goal is not more automation for its own sake. The goal is a quieter operating layer where people can ask better questions, compare stronger answers, and retain control over the reasoning process.
